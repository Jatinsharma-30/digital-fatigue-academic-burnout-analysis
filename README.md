# Digital Fatigue & Academic Burnout — Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Digital devices play an important role in students' academic and everyday lives. However, prolonged screen exposure combined with academic pressure may contribute to digital fatigue, stress, mental exhaustion, and academic burnout.

This project applies Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to questionnaire-based student data to examine digital usage patterns, symptoms of digital fatigue, academic stress, sleep, motivation, and burnout.

The project focuses on identifying meaningful patterns and relationships within the collected responses through data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualization.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze students' digital device usage patterns.
- Examine common symptoms associated with digital fatigue.
- Understand academic stress and burnout among students.
- Study sleep patterns and academic motivation.
- Explore relationships between digital fatigue and academic productivity.
- Identify relationships among stress, motivation, mental exhaustion, sleep, and academic burnout.
- Present meaningful insights through data visualization and statistical analysis.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset was collected through a structured questionnaire designed to study digital fatigue and academic burnout among students.

The questionnaire covers areas including:

- Student profile
- Mode of study
- Device usage
- Daily screen time
- Purpose of digital usage
- Online activity
- Digital fatigue symptoms
- Academic workload and stress
- Sleep duration
- Sleep quality
- Digital usage before sleep
- Academic burnout
- Mental exhaustion
- Academic motivation
- Academic productivity
- Methods for reducing digital fatigue and burnout

**Number of responses:** 72  
**Number of variables:** 30

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning

The dataset was prepared for analysis through:

- Missing-value analysis
- Duplicate-value checking
- Standardization of categorical responses
- Preparation of Likert-scale variables
- Creation of analytical variables
- Data consistency checks

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics were used to understand the overall distribution and characteristics of the collected responses.

### 3. Respondent Profile

The respondent profile was explored using relevant academic and demographic characteristics to understand the composition of the sample.

### 4. Digital Usage Analysis

Digital usage patterns were analyzed based on:

- Daily screen time
- Primary purpose of device usage
- Online activity hours
- Primary device used
- Digital device usage before sleep

### 5. Digital Fatigue Analysis

The project examined different indicators of digital fatigue, including:

- Eye strain
- Headaches
- Impact of headaches on productivity
- Difficulty focusing after prolonged screen exposure
- Break-taking habits
- Awareness of negative effects of prolonged screen usage

### 6. Academic Burnout & Well-being

Academic well-being was examined using:

- Academic burnout rating
- Academic stress
- Mental exhaustion
- Academic motivation
- Sleep duration
- Sleep quality
- Impact of digital fatigue on academic productivity

### 7. Relationship & Correlation Analysis

Relationships between important academic and well-being variables were explored using visualizations and correlation analysis.

The analysis examined relationships involving:

- Overall stress
- Academic burnout
- Academic motivation
- Mental exhaustion
- Sleep quality

A correlation heatmap was also used to visualize relationships among selected numerical variables.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis provides insights into the interaction between students' digital habits and academic well-being.

The project explores:

- Students' patterns of daily digital usage
- Common digital fatigue symptoms
- Academic stress and burnout patterns
- Sleep and motivation-related patterns
- Mental exhaustion among students
- Relationships between stress, motivation, sleep, and academic burnout
- The perceived effect of digital fatigue on academic productivity

The detailed findings are available in the `results` folder.

> **Note:** The findings are based on the collected sample and should be interpreted within the context of the study.
