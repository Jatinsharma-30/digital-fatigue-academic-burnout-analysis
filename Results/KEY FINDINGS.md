# Key Findings

This file presents the major observations obtained from the Exploratory Data Analysis of the Digital Fatigue & Academic Burnout dataset.

## Respondent Profile

- The dataset contains responses collected from students participating in the study.
- The respondent profile was examined using relevant academic and demographic characteristics.
- The analysis provides an overview of the composition of the collected sample.

## Digital Usage

- Students reported different levels of daily digital device usage.
- Digital devices were used for both academic and non-academic activities.
- Online activity hours and digital habits were examined to understand overall screen exposure.
- Digital device usage before sleep was also considered as part of the analysis.

## Digital Fatigue

- The analysis examined symptoms such as eye strain, headaches, and difficulty concentrating after prolonged screen exposure.
- The effect of headaches on daily activities and productivity was also explored.
- Break-taking behavior and awareness of the negative effects of prolonged screen exposure were analyzed.

## Academic Burnout & Well-being

- Academic burnout was analyzed using students' self-reported burnout levels.
- Stress, mental exhaustion, academic motivation, sleep duration, and sleep quality were examined as important well-being indicators.
- The analysis provides an overview of how academic demands may interact with students' overall well-being.

## Relationship Analysis

- Relationships between academic burnout and selected academic and well-being variables were explored.
- Overall stress was examined in relation to academic burnout.
- Academic motivation and mental exhaustion were compared with burnout levels.
- Sleep quality was also examined in relation to academic burnout.

## Correlation Analysis

- Correlation analysis was performed on selected numerical variables.
- The correlation matrix and heatmap were used to identify the direction and strength of relationships among important variables.
- These relationships provide additional statistical context to the visual analysis.

## Overall Observation

The EDA provides a structured view of students' digital usage, digital fatigue, academic stress, and burnout.

The findings highlight the importance of considering digital habits together with academic and well-being factors when studying student burnout.

> **Note:** The findings are based on the collected sample and should be interpreted within the scope and limitations of the study.
